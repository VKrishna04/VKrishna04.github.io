Where the credit appears, and why in so many places:

  src/utils/attribution.js  the single source of the values below
  src/components/Footer.jsx the human-visible line at the foot of every page
  <meta name="generator">   and <meta name="attribution">, injected at build
  JSON-LD                   a schema.org WebSite node naming the creator
  robots.txt, humans.txt    plain-text, generated on every build
  llms.txt, /api/*.json     a Credits section and an _attribution object
  NOTICE.txt                this file, served by the deployed site

The build fails if any of these is missing: scripts/pre-build-validation.js
checks the sources, scripts/verify-attribution.js checks dist/ afterwards.
This is not obfuscation and it is not meant to be unbeatable - anyone who owns
the repository can take all of it out. It is spread out so that removing it is
a deliberate act, visible in the diff, rather than something a refactor or a
redesign does by accident.
